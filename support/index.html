What Tempus is

Tempus logs your work in timed blocks and scores how well you predicted how long that work would take. It separates the hours that served a goal you named from the hours that did not, reads your calendar to find the gaps, and can hold distracting apps shut while a session runs. It does the arithmetic and waits; it never decides what you should work on.

Common questions

My iPhone and my Mac disagree about how much I worked

The two devices share one ledger through your iCloud account, so this means sync has stalled. Open Progress — if a device's work has not reached iCloud, Tempus says so there and offers to reconnect. On the Mac, the drop-down from the menu bar says the same thing. Reconnecting relaunches the app; a running block survives it.

Calendar events I make on one device do not appear on another

Tempus does not sync your calendar — the account that owns the calendar does. If you wrote the event into a calendar that only exists on one device (an "On My iPhone" calendar, for example), it will never travel. Settings → New events go in lets you pick a calendar that does.

The shield is not blocking anything

App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time and needs its permission, which Tempus asks for on the Shield tab and nowhere else. If it was refused, the Shield tab says so. Blocking is not available on macOS at all — Apple does not offer the Screen Time API there, at any price. The Mac can watch and say; it cannot stop you.

The apps I picked on my iPhone are not picked on my iPad

Expected, and not a bug. Apple hands apps to Tempus as opaque tokens that only mean something on the device that made them, so a blocklist's name, its websites and its hours travel between your devices but the app choices have to be made once on each. Tempus is never told which apps you picked.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Through your Apple Account settings, not in Tempus — open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before it renews. If you are in a free trial, cancelling before it ends means you are not charged.

I cancelled. Have I lost my work?

No. Nothing is deleted when a subscription ends. Your goals, tasks and every block you logged stay in your own iCloud, and they are all there when you subscribe again.

Why do I need to sign in?

So your subscription belongs to you rather than to a device. The account holds that and an email address, and nothing else — your goals, tasks and blocks stay in your own iCloud and the server has no way to ask for them. There is no password: you sign in with Apple or Google.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. That removes your account and your subscription record from Tempus's server, permanently. It does not remove your own work, which lives in your iCloud and is not mine to delete — for that, delete the app and clear its iCloud data in your device settings. See the privacy policy.

Why does my insight card show short statements instead of proper sentences?

Tempus writes those up using Apple's on-device model, which needs Apple Intelligence: an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or a Mac with Apple silicon, with the feature switched on. On anything else you get the same findings as plain statements — every figure is there, it is just not joined into prose. The card tells you which of those applies on your device.

Can I stop Tempus sending anything anywhere?

There is one switch, in Settings: "Share anonymous usage", which controls the usage statistics. That is the only thing Tempus sends. Your work, and the insights written from it, never leave your devices at all.
